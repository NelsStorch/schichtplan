## Print shift schedule or export PDF

The shift schedule PDF is generated with the event logo (or department / default logo) in your department's color scheme. First choose the layout:

| Layout | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | All shifts per day in a compact format: time, shift name, task, occupancy (e.g. 3/4), and names. Open slots are highlighted in orange. |
| **Poster / sign-up sheet** | Large print, each slot on its own line. Open slots are rendered as dashed lines for manual handwritten signups on site. |
| **Per person** | All volunteers alphabetically with their assigned shifts. Ideal for distribution or checking assignments during the event. |

You can then customize:

- **Days**: Print selected days only.
- **Landscape**: Offers more width when many names appear in a single row.
- **Each day on a new page**
- **Show details**: Category (with color stripe), task description, age limit, and shift leads.
- **Open slots only**: Prints only tasks that still have unfilled vacancies.

Then:

- <Btn>Print</Btn> directly triggers your browser's print dialog.
- <Btn>Download PDF</Btn> saves the document locally, ready to share with the committee or send to a print shop.

Each page displays an updated timestamp (date and time) and page number at the bottom, so you can immediately see if a posted paper schedule is still current.

:::tip[On mobile or tablet]
On mobile devices, <Btn>Print</Btn> opens the PDF. You can then print or share it using your device's native share menu.
:::

Spreadsheet exports are provided as CSV files and open smoothly in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets with proper character encoding.

:::caution[Personal data]
These documents contain names and contact details. Share them only within the OC, do not upload them to publicly accessible folders, and delete them after the event is over.
:::
