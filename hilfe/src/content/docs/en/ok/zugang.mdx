You can review a summary in the OC area under **Access & Answers**: "Allowed to sign up: …" with all required fields and total active custom questions.

:::note[Always applies]
- Signing up always requires an account. A nickname without a password is sufficient.
- The OC and department leadership can assign anyone manually, including people without accounts.
- Anyone who redeems a valid [Access link](/en/ok/zugangs-links/) can sign up, even if they do not meet the standard criteria.
:::

## Who is allowed to sign up?

In Settings, select one of the six access types:

<AccessTypes />

**Association DB (Cevi.DB)** modes strictly check department and unit affiliations synced from Cevi.DB. Self-selected department memberships do not qualify unless the department leadership has manually [confirmed](/en/abteilung/personen/) the person. If your department is not yet linked to Cevi.DB, the form displays a warning: no one will satisfy the criteria except through individual access links.

### Permitting additional departments

When selecting **Multiple departments**, a list of all known departments appears. Your own department is always included; check the boxes for any others. This is ideal for regional jamborees or when neighboring troops assist at your annual fair.

Volunteers from permitted departments can sign up directly. However, they will only appear in your volunteer pool, messaging lists, and manual assignment dropdowns after they have signed up for a shift or redeemed an access link. Make sure to share the event link with the visiting department.

## Restricting to specific units

For all access types except **Public** and **Cevi.DB, all departments**, you can select specific units. Selecting no units allows all units. If you select units for a department, only volunteers from those units can register. For multiple departments, unit filters can be configured per department.

If required units are missing here, department leaders can create them under [Units](/en/abteilung/stufen/).

## Leaders only

Toggle **Leaders only** to restrict signups exclusively to people with a leadership role:

- Without association DB: Leadership in a unit, specified upon login
- With Cevi.DB: Unit leader or department leader according to Cevi.DB

When combined with a unit filter, leadership in any of the selected units satisfies the requirement.

## Mandatory contact details

Select what contact info is required from all volunteers: **Mobile phone**, **Email**, **Date of birth**, **First and last name**. Residential street addresses are not stored in user profiles; if needed, add the [Custom question "Address"](/en/ok/fragen/). If a volunteer's profile is missing a required detail, they are prompted to provide it upon their first signup. For Cevi.DB accounts, most details are imported automatically.

:::tip[Age limits? Require date of birth]
Without a date of birth, the shift planner cannot verify age restrictions. If your event includes shifts requiring helpers to be 16+ or 18+, enable **Date of birth** as a mandatory field.
:::

## Custom questions

If you need extra information such as T-shirt sizes, dietary requirements, or emergency contacts, configure them under [Questions](/en/ok/fragen/). Each question can have an automatic data retention and deletion schedule.
