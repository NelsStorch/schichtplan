The visual appearance of an event can be customized by the OC Lead and department leadership:

## Banner

The banner is the large hero image displayed at the top of the event page and on the department's homepage. Six curated banner presets are available:

- Fair & Festival Tent
- Summer Camp
- Campfire & Forest
- Grill & Catering
- Mountain Cabin
- Winter Forest Gathering

Alternatively, click **Upload custom banner image** to upload your own picture: widescreen format, up to 6 MB. The shift planner automatically applies a subtle dark gradient over the image to ensure the title remains readable. A calm, high-quality photograph works much better than a flyer with heavy text.

## Logo

Under **Event logo**, upload a square logo image. If left blank, the Cevi or default logo is displayed. The logo is featured in the banner and serves as the browser tab favicon.

## Description

The description appears in the banner beneath the title. Share key information that volunteers need to know: meeting location, attire, catering details. On mobile devices, the first three lines are shown initially.

## Department color

The accent color for buttons and highlights applies across the entire department. It is configured by the department leadership under [Settings](/en/abteilung/einstellungen/), not per event.

All changes are saved automatically, and the status bar at the bottom reflects the current save state.
