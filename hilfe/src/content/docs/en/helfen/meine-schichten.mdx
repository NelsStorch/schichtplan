In the bottom navigation bar (or in the header on desktop), tap **My Shifts**. There you will find all your assignments across all departments in one place.

## What you see there

- **Up next**: Your next upcoming shift is highlighted prominently at the top with “today”, “tomorrow”, or “in 5 days”, along with a compact **Shift briefing**.
- **Shift briefing (offline-ready)**: On your next shift (and by tapping *“Shift briefing”* on any other shift), you will see:
  - **Shift leader or day coordinator** and **event contact** with one-tap buttons for **Phone call** or **Email** (if shared by the OC).
  - **Your team**: Who else is on shift with you and how many spots are filled.
  - **Notes & meeting point**: Key instructions at a glance – accessible even in flight mode or without cell coverage.
- **Breaks & consecutive shifts**: If you have multiple shifts on the same day, the timeline (and event page) automatically calculates your **break duration** (`☕ Break between`), **direct back-to-back shifts** (`⚡ Consecutive shift`), or short **handovers**.
- **Upcoming**: All upcoming shifts organized in a chronological timeline by date.
- **Leadership & responsibility**: If you are assigned as shift coordinator or daily supervisor, a badge with a direct link to the planning view will be shown.
- **Completed**: Past shifts, displayed in muted tones.

Tapping the event title takes you straight to the full schedule.

On your department's home page, the **Your next shift** widget shows a quick summary of the same info.

## Live calendar subscription (automatic sync)

With live calendar subscription, your shifts sync automatically to your smartphone or desktop calendar without needing manual entries.

The widget at the top adapts automatically to your device:

- **Google Calendar (recommended for Android, PC & Web)**:
  - <Btn>Subscribe in Google Calendar</Btn>: Opens Google Calendar directly with your personalized subscription. Simply confirm with *“Add”* – done in one click!
  - Alternative manual setup: Click *“Copy link”* in the modal, open Google Calendar in your browser, click **+** next to *“Other calendars”* ➜ *“From URL”*, and paste the link.
- **Apple Calendar (iPhone, iPad, Mac)**:
  - <Btn>Subscribe in Apple Calendar</Btn>: Launches the native Calendar app on your iPhone or Mac. Confirm the system prompt with *“Subscribe”*.
- **Microsoft Outlook & 365**:
  - Supports both 1-click subscription for Outlook Web and Webcal integration for Outlook desktop.
- **“Where is your calendar?” modal**:
  - Click <Btn variant="ghost">Where is your calendar?</Btn> to view all options, copy the URL for other calendar clients (e.g. Thunderbird), or regenerate your private feed URL.
- **Automatic updates**: When you register for a new shift, when timings change, or when you cancel, your calendar app updates the appointments automatically in the background.
- **Resetting your calendar link**: To revoke access from an old device, click *“Reset subscription link”* inside the dialog to generate a new secret URL. Existing subscriptions must then be updated.

## One-time calendar export (snapshot)

If you prefer static, immutable entries instead of a recurring feed:

- <Btn>Google Calendar</Btn> on a shift: Opens Google Calendar directly with prefilled shift details to save.
- <Btn variant="ghost">.ics</Btn> on a shift: Downloads a standalone calendar file (`.ics`) for that single shift.
- <Btn variant="ghost">Export all as .ics</Btn> under “Upcoming” (or in the calendar modal): Downloads all your upcoming shifts in a single `.ics` file.

:::note[Manual downloads do not update automatically]
Standalone `.ics` files and single-shift buttons create a point-in-time snapshot. Rescheduled shifts or cancellations will not update automatically. Use the live calendar subscription for automatic synchronization.
:::

## Also on the main portal

On `cevi-schichtplan.ch/profil` under the **Overview** tab, the **Your next shifts** section highlights upcoming assignments. The **Subscribe to calendar** button provides the same 1-click options for Google Calendar and Apple/Outlook.
