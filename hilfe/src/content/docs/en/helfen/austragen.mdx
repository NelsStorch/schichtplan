## Via “My Shifts”

<Path steps={['My Shifts', 'Upcoming', 'Cancel sign-up']} />

<Steps>
1. Tap **My Shifts** in the bottom navigation (or in the top header on desktop).
2. Locate the shift under **Upcoming**.
3. Tap <Btn variant="danger">Cancel sign-up</Btn> and confirm your cancellation.
</Steps>

## Directly on the event page

Open the shift within the event schedule. Next to your task, it will say **Your task**. Tap <Btn variant="danger">Leave this task</Btn> and confirm.

## Prefer to look for a replacement?

Instead of canceling outright, you can offer your shift to others. You remain assigned until someone takes over, and you receive an email confirmation once someone does. See [Finding a replacement](/en/helfen/ersatz-suchen/).

## Sick or prevented at short notice?

Did you fall ill or can no longer attend due to an unexpected emergency?

- **Cancel as early as possible:** The moment you cancel, the slot in the shift planner turns orange as “open” immediately, allowing other helpers or leaders to step in.
- **Shortly before or during the event:** If canceling on the day of the event or just a few hours prior, please also notify the **OC or your shift leader directly by phone or chat** (contact numbers are found at the top of the event page). This ensures organizers can arrange a replacement in good time.

## Good to know

- You can cancel at any time, unless the OC has enabled a **cancellation lock** (e.g. starting 2 days before the event to stabilize scheduling). If the lock is active, you can [look for a replacement](/en/helfen/ersatz-suchen/) or contact the event coordinators directly so they can cancel your shift manually.
- You can still cancel your own sign-up even if sign-ups for the event have officially closed.
- Past shifts cannot be canceled; they are archived under **Completed**.
- If you want to switch to a different task in the same shift, first cancel your existing task and then sign up for the new one.
- If the OC assigned you manually and you cannot attend, the same applies: cancel your sign-up and give the OC a brief heads-up.
