Some events display an “Attendance Check-in” poster with a QR code at the entrance or festival office. This lets you confirm your arrival quickly and contactlessly for your shift.

1. **Scan the QR code** using your smartphone’s default camera app.
2. **Log in** if you are not already signed in on that browser. You will be redirected back automatically.
3. **Done:** The screen immediately shows the confirmation “You are checked in!” along with shift details and timestamp.

<QrCheckinMock />

## When check-in works

Check-in is active **from 30 minutes before your shift starts until its scheduled end**.
- **Arrived too early?** The screen will show when your next shift begins. Simply scan the code again closer to your start time.
- **Not signed up?** You must be assigned to the shift. If you are helping out spontaneously, talk to the OC: they can assign you and mark you present on the spot.

## Frequently asked questions about check-in

- **Do I need a special app?** No, the regular camera app on your smartphone is all you need.
- **“This QR code is no longer valid”:** The OC regenerated the code for security reasons. Ask at the festival desk or OC for the updated poster.
- **No mobile reception at the venue?** No problem: The OC can manually mark you as “Present” in their attendance list at any time.
