If you cannot attend a shift, you do not have to cancel outright. With the built-in **Find replacement** swap feature, you can offer your shift for others to take over. Anyone authorized to register for the event can accept your shift with a single click.

Shift swapping works even when the [cancellation lock](/en/helfen/austragen/#good-to-know) for the event is active.

## Finding a replacement

<Path steps={['My Shifts', 'Upcoming', 'Find replacement']} />

<Steps>
1. Tap **My Shifts** and locate the shift under **Upcoming**. Alternatively, open the shift directly on the event page.
2. Tap <Btn>Find replacement</Btn>.
3. Optionally add a short note, e.g. “On vacation this weekend”.
4. Confirm with <Btn>Find replacement</Btn>.
</Steps>

The reason is visible to anyone who can see names on the schedule. Avoid personal details, such as medical conditions.

## What happens next

- **You remain assigned** until someone takes over. Until then, the shift remains your responsibility.
- In the shift planner, the shift is marked with **Replacement requested**, showing your name and note.
- **As soon as someone takes over**, your assignment is cleared and you receive an email notification. This requires a verified email address on your account.
- If no one steps up, you stay registered. Please get in touch with the OC in good time.
- Changed your plans? Tap <Btn variant="secondary">Cancel replacement request</Btn> (or <Btn variant="secondary">Withdraw</Btn> on the event page).
- **Spread the word:** Open the shift in the event view and tap <Btn variant="secondary">Share request</Btn>. A preview saying “Replacement requested” with date and time will be generated for WhatsApp & social platforms, without displaying your name.

## Taking over a shift

<Steps>
1. On the event page, shifts with replacement requests appear at the top under **Still looking for** and carry the **Replacement requested** tag. Use the filter **Replacement requested** to display only these.
2. Open the shift and tap <Btn>Take over</Btn> next to the helper's name.
3. Confirm. If the shift requires missing information or answers to custom questions, the system will prompt you.
</Steps>

You take over that exact task. The same criteria as regular sign-ups apply: event access permissions, [minimum age](/en/helfen/mindestalter/), and no conflicting overlaps with your other shifts. Taking over also works when general registration for the event is already closed.

## Good to know

- Finding and taking over replacements only works if enabled by the OC (enabled by default), and only up to the event deadline: until shift start or several hours prior.
- The OC can also initiate a replacement search on your behalf, for example following a phone call.
- The OC sees who is looking for a replacement in the dashboard and can trace who took over which shift in the activity log.
