Some shifts or tasks enforce a minimum age requirement – for example, to comply with Swiss legal youth protection laws regarding alcohol service (beer and wine from 16, spirits or bar supervision from 18). You can spot such shifts by clear badges such as **16+** or **Minimum age 18 years**.

## Three types of age limits

| Restriction | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shift from age X | Everyone in the shift must be at least X years old | Night shift 18+ |
| Task from age X, **all helpers** | Everyone in this task must be at least X years old | Bar service 16+ |
| Task from age X, **min. 1 in shift** | At least one person in the shift must be at least X | Bar supervision: one helper 18+ |

With “min. 1 in shift”, younger helpers are free to sign up. However, the **final available spot** is reserved for someone meeting the age requirement if no older helper has enrolled yet.

## How age is calculated

The shift planner calculates your age on the **date of the shift**, not the day you sign up. If you turn 16 a week before the village fair, you can register for the bar shift today.

If you do not meet the age requirement, a message will appear: “All helpers for the Bar task must be at least 16 years old.”

## Where your date of birth comes from

- **With Cevi.DB**: pulled from your Cevi.DB profile and editable only there.
- **With nickname or email**: the date provided during login or in your [profile](/en/helfen/konto/).

If your date of birth is missing and a shift requires an age verification, the system will prompt for it when signing up. You cannot sign up without providing your date of birth.

:::note[For the OC]
If you manually assign someone whose date of birth is not recorded, the shift planner cannot check their age automatically. Compliance remains your responsibility. If you need birth dates for all participants, turn on the required **Date of birth** field under [Access & permissions](/en/ok/zugang/).
:::

## For the OC: youth protection alert

In the OC dashboard under **To do**, a **Youth protection** alert appears if a task requiring “at least 1 person” lacks a qualifying adult. Under **Shifts**, the warning is highlighted directly next to the task. Each task clearly indicates “Age requirement met” or “No helper aged 18+ assigned yet”. Volunteer ages are exclusively visible to the OC.
