After logging in for the first time, a yellow notification appears at the top: **“Which organization do you belong to?”**. It stays visible until you have made a selection. Choosing “No organization” is also a valid response.

## Choosing organization & groups

<Steps>
1. Tap <Btn>Specify organization</Btn>. You are taken to the selection page.
2. Select your department, unit, or club from the list. The search field helps filter long lists.
3. If you do not belong to a permanent unit (e.g. as a guest or one-time volunteer), select **I do not belong to any organization**.
4. If groups or units are listed (e.g. leader team, active squad, juniors), select your role for each group (**No**, **Participant / Member**, or **Leader**).
5. Tap <Btn variant="primary">Save and continue</Btn>. Your settings take effect immediately.
</Steps>

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="For Cevi / Pfadi / Jubla (Association)">
    If you signed in with your association database (Cevi.DB, MiData, or Jubla-DB), your department or unit is usually **assigned automatically** based on your active leadership roles and does not need to be selected manually. Official association groups and units are also synchronized directly.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Via invitation or department link">
    If you follow an **invitation link** (or email invite), you are enrolled directly into the invited department or club – without choosing from a list. If you access via a department's direct web address (`<name>.…`), that department is already pre-selected for you during setup.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Passwordless login">
    If the department has enabled passwordless sign-in, you can log in on their page with your nickname and birth date and are directly assigned to that department.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Changing settings later

<Path steps={['Account', 'Profile & Settings', 'Overview', 'Department & Units']} />

Tap <Btn>Change</Btn> there. For clubs this card is labeled “Club & Units”, for Jubla “Unit & Groups”.

## Why this matters

- Events set to **Internal** only admit members of that specific organization.
- Events with a **group filter** only permit specific teams (e.g. only the leadership team or the 1st team).
- Events marked **Leaders only** require the **Leader** role in at least one group.
- If an organization's portal page is configured as [members-only](/en/abteilung/einstellungen/), volunteers can only view the events if they are linked to that organization.
