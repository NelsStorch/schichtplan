The shift planner replaces messy Excel spreadsheets, chat messages, and Doodle polls for camps, festivals, village fairs (Chilbis), and tournaments. Volunteers see at a glance where help is needed and sign up directly. The organizing committee (OC) sees in real time which shifts are filled.

Each unit has its own permanent web address, for example `illnau.cevi-schichtplan.ch` or `turnier.vereins-schichtplan.ch`. This address remains permanently valid and fits perfectly on flyers or in helper invitations.

:::tip[One login is enough]
You log in once and are signed in across all units of your organization. If you help out at a neighboring department or another club, you do not need a second account.
:::

## How everything connects

- **The organization:**
  - In **Cevi** and **Pfadi**, each **department (Abteilung)** has its own shift planner, managed by the **department leadership (AL)**.
  - In **Jubla**, each **unit (Schar)** has its shift planner, managed by the **unit leadership (SL)**.
  - For **clubs and associations**, each **club** (or committee) has its shift planner, managed by the **club leadership or board**.
- An organization hosts any number of **events**, such as “Chilbi 2026”, “Summer Camp”, or “Club Tournament”.
- An event has **shifts**, for example “Saturday 17:00 to 20:00”.
- A shift contains one or more **tasks (roles)** with specific helper slots, for example Grill (2 slots), Bar (2 slots), Cashier (1 slot).
- The **organizing committee (OC)** plans the shifts of an event. The leadership manages the entire organization.

More terms are explained in the [Glossary](/en/wissen/glossar/).

## On smartphones

The shift planner is designed mobile-first from the ground up. At the bottom, a fixed navigation bar provides quick access to **Events**, **My Shifts**, and **Account**. On desktop computers, these same links appear in the top header.

You can add your organization’s page to your home screen (select “Add to Home Screen” in your smartphone browser). It will then launch like a native app, without needing an app store download.

## On computers & keyboard shortcuts

On a laptop or PC, a quick search bar is located in the top navigation. Pressing the keyboard shortcut <kbd>⌘K</kbd> (Mac) or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (Windows) opens the [Command Palette](/en/wissen/befehlsmenue/) to jump between events, search shifts, or assign helpers without touching the mouse.
