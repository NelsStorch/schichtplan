For youth associations, the shift planner connects directly to the official association databases (Hitobito). Independent clubs manage their members and leadership roles autonomously right within the shift planner.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="For associations (Cevi.DB, MiData, Jubla-DB)">
    The platforms **Cevi.DB** (`db.cevi.ch`), **PBS MiData** (`db.scout.ch`), and **Jubla-Datenbank** (`db.jubla.ch`) are built on the proven open-source association software **Hitobito**.

    ### What the shift planner syncs

    | From association database | In the shift planner |
    | --- | --- |
    | Association nickname (Cevi name, scout name, leader name) | Display name on the shift schedule |
    | First and last name | Official identity verification |
    | Date of birth | Automatic age verification for shifts (cannot be manipulated by users) |
    | Email address | System notifications and shift confirmations |
    | Group hierarchy (local group, department, troop) | Permanent home department |
    | Roles in units and groups | Allocation to [units](/en/abteilung/stufen/) as leader or participant |
    | Department or troop leadership role | Automatic leadership and administration rights |

    For exact details on which groups and roles qualify, including for regions and cantonal associations, see [Groups & roles](/en/abteilung/gruppen-und-rollen/).

    :::note[The shift planner only reads]
    The shift planner strictly reads data from the association database. It never alters or overwrites data in your association database.
    :::

    ### When data is updated

    Data synchronization occurs automatically upon **every login**. If details change in the association database (e.g. appointment to department leadership or moving to another unit), they take effect the next time that person logs in to the shift planner.

    :::tip[Role newly assigned?]
    If someone has just been assigned a new role in the association database, they simply need to **log out and log in again** in the shift planner. Permissions are stored in the login token and are updated immediately.
    :::
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="For clubs (Autonomous management)">
    Independent clubs, musical societies, sports clubs, and festival committees do **not need an external association database**:

    - **Email & password:** Members and leaders register easily with their email address and password.
    - **Permissions & roles:** The board flexibly assigns planning and administrative permissions under [Members & permissions](/en/abteilung/rechte/).
    - **Groups & teams:** Under [Units & groups](/en/abteilung/stufen/), you can set up custom club teams (e.g. Active Team, Board, Seniors).
    - **Access links:** For helpers who do not need a permanent club account, the OC simply generates direct [Access links](/en/ok/zugangs-links/).
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Data privacy & data minimization

For data privacy reasons, the shift planner **never** imports residential addresses or sensitive personal records. If the OC requires additional information for an event (e.g. an emergency contact number or a T-shirt size), this is requested as a specific [Custom question in the event](/en/ok/fragen/).
