Whether you are a youth organization or a local sports club: Every organization gets its own permanent web address (subdomain) on the shift planner, for example `illnau.cevi-schichtplan.ch` or `tournament.vereins-schichtplan.ch`.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="For Cevi / Scout / Jubla (Association)">
    ## Who is allowed to create the shift planner?

    In youth associations, the shift planner is created by the **official leadership according to the association database**:
    - **Cevi:** Role "Abteilungsleiter*in" (Department Leader) in Cevi.DB
    - **Pfadi / Scouts:** Role "Abteilungsleitung" (Group Leader) in PBS MiData
    - **Jubla:** Role "Scharleitung" (Troop Leader) in the Jubla database

    **Regions, cantonal associations, and national boards** (for Cevi: sections, member organizations, and the umbrella organization) can also create their own shift planner through their leadership. You can see which roles qualify under [Groups & roles](/en/abteilung/gruppen-und-rollen/), and how to plan with them under [Region, canton & national](/en/abteilung/ebenen/).

    This ensures that exactly one official shift planner exists per group.

    ## How to create one for association departments

    <Steps>
    1. Open your association's main website (e.g. `cevi-schichtplan.ch` or `pfadi-schichtplan.ch`) and tap <Btn variant="primary">Create department</Btn>.
    2. Sign in with your association account (Cevi.DB, MiData, or Jubla-DB).
    3. If your department does not have a shift planner yet, the system recognizes your leadership role and offers to create it.
    4. **Name**: Proposed automatically from the association database (e.g. "Cevi Weiningen" or "Pfadi St. Georg").
    5. **Subdomain**: The short web address (e.g. `weiningen` for `weiningen.cevi-schichtplan.ch`). 3 to 30 characters. **Cannot be changed later.**
    6. Check the box for the **Terms of Service**. You agree to them on behalf of your department, including the data processing terms. Without checking the box, the department cannot be created.
    7. Click <Btn variant="primary">Create department</Btn>. You will immediately be redirected to your new shift planner.
    </Steps>

    ### What happens next
    - You automatically become the **main responsible leader** in the shift planner.
    - The department is linked directly to your official association group.
    - Other leaders in your group automatically receive leadership permissions upon logging in.
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="For clubs (Email & password)">
    ## Who is allowed to create a club?

    For independent clubs, sports teams, and festival committees, **any board or organizing committee (OC) member** can set up a new club in the shift planner. The creator automatically becomes the main administrator and can subsequently grant permissions to other people in the club.

    ## How to set up a club

    <Steps>
    1. Open `vereins-schichtplan.ch` and tap <Btn variant="primary">Create club</Btn>.
    2. Sign in with your email account or create a free new club account.
    3. **Club name**: Enter the official name of your club (e.g. "FC Musterstadt" or "Turnverein Wald").
    4. **Subdomain**: Choose your personal web address (e.g. `fcmusterstadt` for `fcmusterstadt.vereins-schichtplan.ch`).
    5. Check the box for the **Terms of Service**. You agree to them on behalf of your club, including the data processing terms. Without checking the box, the club cannot be created.
    6. Click <Btn variant="primary">Create club</Btn>. Your club portal is immediately ready for use.
    </Steps>

    ### What to do next
    - In [Settings](/en/abteilung/einstellungen/), upload the club logo and set your brand colors.
    - Create [groups or teams](/en/abteilung/stufen/) (e.g. Active Team, Board, Juniors).
    - Grant leadership permissions to additional board members under [Members & permissions](/en/abteilung/rechte/).
    - [Create your first event](/en/ok/anlass-erstellen/).
  </TabItem>

## Frequently asked questions

<FaqItem q="«A shift planner already exists for your organization»">
Someone on your team has already created the organization. Contact your main leadership to obtain administrator or planning permissions for your portal.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="«Only the department leadership can create a new department» (Association)">
Your official department or troop leadership role has not yet been registered in the association database. Please contact your association database administrator to update your role.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Can the subdomain be changed later?">
For technical reasons, the subdomain (e.g. <code>muster.vereins-schichtplan.ch</code>) cannot be renamed once created, as it is anchored on flyers and links. Therefore, choose a concise, recognizable name.
</FaqItem>
