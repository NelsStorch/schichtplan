The shift planner only displays as much information as necessary for effective event planning. Contact information is strictly restricted to the team organizing the event.

## At a glance

| Detail | Other volunteers | Event OC | Department leadership | Platform admins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nickname or display name on schedule | yes¹ | yes | yes | yes |
| Mobile number, email | no | yes | yes | yes |
| Date of birth and age | no | yes | yes | yes |
| Answers to custom questions (e.g. address), until retention expiry | no | yes | yes | yes |
| Department and group memberships | no | under “Helpers” | yes | yes |
| Your shifts with other departments | no | no | no | yes |

¹ For **Public** events and **Cevi.DB, all departments**, names are visible to anyone viewing the schedule. For internal events, only participants eligible to register can see helper names; all others see the schedule without names.

## Who belongs to the OC?

Organizers assigned to an event's OC are listed at the top of the event with their respective committee roles. In addition, department leadership and individuals granted the “View all events” permission can view organizer details. See [Roles and permissions](/en/ok/rollen/).

## What happens to CSV exports?

The OC can download participant lists as CSV files, for instance to prepare emergency contact sheets on site. From that moment, the file is held by the OC. Organizers are asked to delete these local files once the event concludes.

## Data imported from Cevi.DB

When signing in with Cevi.DB, the shift planner imports select basic details from your profile. Details on which fields are synchronized can be found under [Association databases (Hitobito)](/en/abteilung/hitobito/). The shift planner never writes data back to Cevi.DB.

## Account deletion

Accounts created with Cevi.DB or password can be deleted [directly by yourself](/en/helfen/konto/#deleting-your-account). Your personal details and survey responses are erased, while past shift records are anonymized as “Deleted person”. Nickname-only profiles submit a deletion request from the same tab and are cleared within 30 days. Accounts inactive for two years are automatically [purged](/en/helfen/konto/#inactive-accounts).

You can download a complete export of all data stored about your account at any time via **Download my data** on the profile page.
