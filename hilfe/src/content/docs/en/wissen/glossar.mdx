title: Glossary
description: The most important terms in the shift planner, explained clearly.
tableOfContents: false
---

<dl class="glossary">
  <dt>Department / Unit / Club (Abteilung / Schar / Verein)</dt>
  <dd>The local organization with its own dedicated web address, such as <code>illnau.cevi-schichtplan.ch</code>, <code>scout.pfadi-schichtplan.ch</code>, <code>olten.jubla-schichtplan.ch</code>, or <code>turnier.vereins-schichtplan.ch</code>. Contains events, units/groups, and volunteers.</dd>

  <dt>Department Leadership (AL) / Unit Leadership (SL) / Club Board</dt>
  <dd>Administrative role with full management permissions for the respective organization in the shift planner.</dd>

  <dt>Event (Anlass)</dt>
  <dd>A planned event featuring shifts, such as “Chilbi 2026”, “Summer Camp”, or “Club Tournament”.</dd>

  <dt>Task / Role (Arbeit)</dt>
  <dd>The specific job within a shift, for example Grill, Bar, Cashier, or Parking Patrol. Has allocated volunteer slots and minimum age limits.</dd>

  <dt>Task Catalog (Arbeiten-Katalog)</dt>
  <dd>A reusable catalog of common tasks across your organization. See <a href="/en/ok/arbeiten-katalog/">Task Catalog</a>.</dd>

  <dt>Setup &amp; Teardown (Auf- &amp; Abbau)</dt>
  <dd>A badge for shifts occurring before or after the main event. Volunteers can filter specifically for these setup shifts.</dd>

  <dt>Command Palette (Befehlsmenü)</dt>
  <dd>Press <code>⌘K</code> or <code>Ctrl+K</code> (or the search icon) to open the quick launcher to search shifts, assign volunteers, switch between events, or copy links without using the mouse. See <a href="/en/wissen/befehlsmenue/">Command Palette &amp; Quick Search</a>.</dd>

  <dt>Cevi.DB / PBS MiData / Jubla-DB</dt>
  <dd>The official member and leader databases of Cevi (<code>db.cevi.ch</code>), Scouts (<code>db.scout.ch</code>), and Jubla (<code>db.jubla.ch</code>). Signing in with them automatically synchronizes names, leadership roles, and groups.</dd>

  <dt>Cevi Name / Scout Name / Leader Name / Nickname</dt>
  <dd>The camp nickname or call sign. Combined with date of birth, it enables lightning-fast passwordless sign-in.</dd>

  <dt>Shift Swap / Find Replacement (Ersatz suchen)</dt>
  <dd>Offer an assigned shift to others. Other eligible volunteers can take over your shift with a single click. See <a href="/en/helfen/ersatz-suchen/">Finding a Replacement</a>.</dd>

  <dt>Manual Assignment (Einteilen)</dt>
  <dd>The OC directly assigns a person to a shift rather than waiting for them to sign up themselves.</dd>

  <dt>Home Department / Primary Club (Heimabteilung)</dt>
  <dd>The primary organization you belong to. Derived automatically from leadership roles for association accounts, or chosen manually.</dd>

  <dt>Hitobito</dt>
  <dd>The Swiss open-source community platform powering Cevi.DB, PBS MiData, and Jubla-DB. See <a href="/en/abteilung/hitobito/">Association Databases &amp; Clubs</a>.</dd>

  <dt>Helpers / Volunteers (Helfende)</dt>
  <dd>All people registered for shifts or belonging to the volunteer community. See <a href="/en/ok/helfer-pool/">Volunteer Pool and Quotas</a>.</dd>

  <dt>OC (Organizing Committee)</dt>
  <dd>The event organizing team, consisting of the event director and department heads.</dd>

  <dt>OC Dashboard (OK-Bereich)</dt>
  <dd>The management area for event organizers: Overview, Shifts, Helpers, Messages, Attendance, Export, and Settings. See <a href="/en/ok/ok-bereich/">The OC Dashboard</a>.</dd>

  <dt>Local Unit / Troop (Ortsgruppe / Schar)</dt>
  <dd>The official organizational entity in the association database linked to the shift planner organization.</dd>

  <dt>Permissions (Rechte)</dt>
  <dd>Capabilities granted within an organization, such as “Create events”. Role templates like “Administration” bundle multiple permissions together. See <a href="/en/abteilung/rechte/">Permissions in the Department</a>.</dd>

  <dt>Platform Administrator</dt>
  <dd>Responsible for overall technical maintenance of the shift planner platform across all organizations.</dd>

  <dt>Committee Role (Ressort)</dt>
  <dd>Designation of an OC responsibility, for example “Finances”, “Catering”, or “Infrastructure”.</dd>

  <dt>Shift (Schicht)</dt>
  <dd>A specific time window during an event, for example “Saturday 17:00 to 20:00”. Contains one or more tasks.</dd>

  <dt>Shift Quota (Schicht-Soll)</dt>
  <dd>Recommended minimum number of shifts per helper for an event, serving as a guideline for organizers.</dd>

  <dt>Slug</dt>
  <dd>The trailing part of an event URL, for example <code>chilbi-2026</code> in <code>…/events/chilbi-2026</code>.</dd>

  <dt>Unit / Group (Stufe / Gruppe)</dt>
  <dd>A team or subdivision within the organization, such as a leader team, youth unit, or sports section.</dd>

  <dt>Subdomain</dt>
  <dd>The prefix of an organization's web address, for example <code>illnau</code> in <code>illnau.cevi-schichtplan.ch</code>.</dd>

  <dt>Template (Vorlage)</dt>
  <dd>A saved event layout without helper assignments, ready to be duplicated for recurring annual events. See <a href="/en/ok/vorlagen/">Templates</a>.</dd>

  <dt>Access Link (Zugangs-Link)</dt>
  <dd>A dedicated invitation link generated by the OC that grants people direct sign-up access to internal shifts. See <a href="/en/ok/zugangs-links/">Access Links</a>.</dd>
</dl>
