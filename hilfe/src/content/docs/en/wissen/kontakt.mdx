The shift planner is a volunteer-run non-profit project. Most operational questions can be answered much faster by your event OC or department leadership.

## Who should I ask?

| Question | Best contact |
| --- | --- |
| Event schedule, venue, what to bring, shift swaps | The event **OC** (listed at the top of the event page) |
| Incorrect department, group roles, portal locked | Your **department leadership** |
| Incorrect profile data synced from Cevi.DB | The **Cevi.DB administrator** of your department |
| Forgotten password, platform bugs, technical issues | The platform **operators**, see below |
| Deleting a nickname-only profile | On your profile page under **Security & Data** via **Request deletion** |

When reporting a bug, please include:

- The exact URL of the page where the issue occurred
- What you did and what you expected to happen
- A screenshot of any error message
- Device type (mobile/desktop) and browser name

Never include passwords in bug reports.
